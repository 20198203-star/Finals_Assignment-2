ABOUT NEW YORK

Nicknamed “The City That Never Sleeps,” New York City is one of the most popular destinations in the world. And there are so many things to do in NYC that the list is about as long as 5th Avenue.

Top 10 New York City tourist attractions you shouldn’t miss

Explore the top places to visit in New York City. From Broadway and the Statue of Liberty to the Empire State Building, here’s what to do in NYC.

Broadway

Seeing a Broadway show is a classic New York City experience. You’ll get to watch some of the world’s best performers in action, with incredible music, sets, and costumes — it’s easy to see why this is one of the most popular New York City tourist attractions. Broadway is all about top-notch theater, and you’re sure to leave with memories you’ll be talking about long after the final curtain.

The Empire State Building

One of the top places to visit in New York is undoubtedly the Empire State Building, where you can get some of the best views of NYC. Previously the tallest building in the world, the Empire State Building stands a whopping 1,250 feet tall. Scaling this famous 102-story skyscraper is one of the best things to do in New York, and you’ll be rewarded with panoramic vistas over the whole city from not one but two observation decks.

The Statue of Liberty

It’s safe to say that, of all the attractions in New York City, the Statue of Liberty is the most iconic. Standing tall on an island in New York Harbor, Lady Liberty was originally a gift from France to commemorate their alliance with the USA during the American Revolution. You can sail by the Statue of Liberty on a NYC boat tour, or climb all the way up the copper crown with an advanced reservation and combine your visit with a stop at Ellis Island.

Central Park

You can’t visit New York and not spend some time roaming around Central Park. Home to gardens, waterfalls, bridges, and even music performances, this bustling green space is truly the heart of the city. Central Park is the most visited urban park in the US, and however you choose to experience it, spending some time here is definitely one of the best things to do in NYC.

9/11 Memorial and Museum

The 9/11 Memorial & Museum is one of the must-visit places in New York City, as it honors the victims of the 1993 and 2001 terror attacks on the World Trade Center. There are two waterfalls which mark where the Twin Towers once stood — the largest of their kind in the USA. The memorial is free to visit, and is one of the most moving attractions in NYC.

THINGS TO DO IN NEW YORK CITY

Check out the Chelsea Market

The Chelsey Market is exactly what it sounds like: a cool market in the Chelsea district of NYC. There are tons of cool restaurants, shops, and places to spend your money at which makes it attractive and scary at the time. If you go by, definitely check Los Tacos #1 or Los Mariscos for Tacos, both are great. If not in the mood for Mexican, try Lobster Place which is also exactly what it sounds like!

Eat a Red Velvet Cake, Black & White Cookie and/or NY Cheesecake

Neither of these dishes is native to NYC, but both have gone pro in this most metropolitan of cities. Where do you go to try one—or ideally both—of these dishes? Lili’s Bake Shop on 7th & 53rd. We also like Veniero’s, City Cakes, and Buttercup Bake Shop. Their specialty is the Dirty Cheesecake which is delicious, but you could cross cheesecake, red velvet, and the famous black-and-white cookie off your list here. D-LISH!

Visit the MoMA

The Museum of Modern Art (MoMA) is one of NYC’s leading museums and arguably the world’s greatest modern art collection. The gallery itself is a work of art that is only out-shadowed by its prestigious collection of over 200,000 pieces. You’ll find everything from Picasso to Jackson Pollock and Mark Rothko here.

Pizza by the Slice

Pizza is unequivocally an Italian dish that is best eaten in Italy. That said, as a person who has lived in Italy and NYC, pizza-by-the-slice is an NYC specialty that is best eaten in NYC. Where? Joe’s Pizza has a few stores in NYC and is regarded as the best pizza by the slice in the city. Visit it and decide for yourself!
